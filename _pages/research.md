## Research Overview

My research sits at the intersection of computational proteomics and artificial intelligence/machine learning, with a focus on converting complex biological and omics datasets into interpretable discoveries and practical health technologies.

My work has spanned machine-learning approaches for proteomics and transcriptomic data, including explainable AI and symbolic regression for identifying genetic targets in perturbed stem cell systems, as well as AI-enabled clinical tools such as ML-driven prognostic devices and patient-support platforms.

---

## Research Areas

### 1. Dynamic Precision Medicine: Georgetown University ICBI

**Dynamic Precision Medicine Laboratory (DPM Lab)**  
*Research on mechanistic ODE models and evolutionary classifiers for virtual patient simulation*

Developing machine learning algorithms and pipelines on large-scale virtual patient simulation datasets. Investigating the implications of Dynamic Precision Medicine (DPM) and mechanistic ODEs for evolutionary classifiers. This work explores how mechanistic models can be combined with patient data, including large simulated patient datasets, to enable personalized treatment planning.

---

### 2. Computational Proteomics & Protein Language Models

**Interpretability of Protein Language Models**  
*Testing whether features learned by protein language models reflect real biological signal*

Investigating what protein language models learn and whether those features correspond to actual biological mechanisms. Using controlled ablation studies, changing or removing parts of model input and measuring output effects, rather than relying on feature visualization alone. Focus on protein stability modeling and chaperone-dependent protein folding.

---

### 3. GeneLab for High Schools / Biology & Capstone Research Program

**Explainable AI and Symbolic Regression Identify Genetic Targets for Restoring Perturbed Arabidopsis Root Stem Cell Development Under Microgravity**  
*Selected to present at the "American Society for Gravitational Space Research (ASGSR) 2026"*

Transcriptomics ML/AI research project selected as top 40 teams out of 1,000+ participants. Applied explainable AI and symbolic regression to identify genetic targets for restoring perturbed *Arabidopsis thaliana* root stem cell development under microgravity conditions. Research abstract selected for presentation at the American Society for Gravitational and Space Research (ASGSR) annual meeting.

---

### 4. [CodeTheCure: International Scale Cancer Technology Organization](https://codethecure.app)

**[Co-founder & Chief Technology Officer](https://codethecure.app/team)**  

Leading a software development team of ~15 developers building and maintaining RAG-powered AI software and multiple products across our ecosystem serving 300+ monthly active cancer patients and 3,000+ yearly active users across 4+ software apps. Partnered/sponsored by [American Cancer Society (SC)](https://codethecure.app/about), [Microsoft Azure](https://codethecure.app/about), [PMAC UK](https://codethecure.app/about), and helped lead the CodeTheCure International Hackathon ([MIT THINK](https://codethecure.app/about), [n8n partnered/sponsored with us](https://codethecure.app/about)).

**Products:**
- [**Juniper**](https://juniper.codethecure.app): Symptom-aware care calendar for treatment tracking
- [**KeyCog**](https://keycog.codethecure.app): ML-based CICI (Chemotherapy-Induced Cognitive Impairment) detection via keystroke dynamics. Features the team-designed **Temporal Dynamics Engine**, an Isolation Forest Model that analyzes typing dynamics and rhythms to estimate chemotherapy-induced cognitive impairment trajectory and automatically adapt UI/UX to the user's current cognitive state.
- [**Rivertide**](https://codethecure.github.io/rivertide-website/): Voice-as-an-OS productivity hub for cancer patients
- [**Leafy**](https://codethecure.app/leafy): Voice-to-text writing assistant for cancer patients experiencing chemo brain and brain fog. Free, open source, runs locally on your Mac.

### 5. MelanoScan: A Cutting-Edge Diagnostic Tool for Early Melanoma Detection

**ML-based Diagnostic Device**  
*Arduino-based metabolic assessment with computer vision*

Collected 520+ skin lesion images from ISIC database and fine-tuned a Python Computer Vision model integrated with an Arduino-based Metabolic Assessment Device, achieving 81.25% accuracy. Won 1st place school-wide and 3rd place county-wide Science Fair (Biomedical Engineering).
