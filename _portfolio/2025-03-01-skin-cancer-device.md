Machine-Learning-based skin cancer prognostic device through metabolism analysis. Collected 520+ skin lesion images from ISIC database and fine-tuned a Python Computer Vision model integrated with Arduino-based Metabolic Assessment Device, achieving 81.25% accuracy. Won 1st place school-wide and 3rd place county-wide Science Fair (Biomedical Engineering).
