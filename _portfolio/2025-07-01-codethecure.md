Co-founded the first-ever RAG-powered AI software to make oncology information accessible for >300 monthly cancer patients. Led development team of ~15 developers. Over ~3000 yearly active users. Partnered/sponsored by American Cancer Society (SC), Microsoft Azure, MIT THINK, n8n. Products: Juniper (symptom-aware care calendar), KeyCog (ML-based CICI detection via keystroke dynamics), Rivertide (Voice-as-an-OS productivity hub for cancer patients).
